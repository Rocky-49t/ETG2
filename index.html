    Rules of the Goanrooms

    • Sound travels. Sprinting, gunfire and slamming doors draw things toward you. Crouch to move silently.
    • Darkness eats your mind. Sanity drains in the dark and near entities. Low sanity shows you things that aren't there — usually.
    • Drink, eat, patch up. Almond water restores thirst and calms nerves. Bandages heal over time.
    • Every thing has a weakness. Fire scares the hounds. Light stuns what walks in ink. Water hides what you should fear.
    • Hide. Lockers and wardrobes keep you out of sight — if nothing saw you climb in.
    • Read the walls. Spray-painted EXIT arrows sit beside the doorway that leads toward the nearest way out. Follow them from room to room.
    • Feel the draft. Every exit breathes cold air you can hear. Levels have more than one way out: holes in the floor, and hatches you can force open (loudly) or pry with a crowbar.
    • Together. In co-op, downed friends can be revived by holding E.
